GST Rate on Clothes in India: 5% up to ₹2,500, 18% Above

Short answer: since 22 September 2025, readymade garments sold at up to ₹2,500 per piece attract 5% GST, and garments above ₹2,500 per piece attract 18%.

How the ₹2,500 limit works

  • The limit is applied per piece, not on the whole bill.
  • It is tested on the transaction value, meaning the price actually charged, excluding GST.
  • On one invoice, a ₹1,800 shirt and a ₹4,000 jacket fall in different rates, so every line needs its own rate.

Worked examples

Kurta₹1,8005%₹90.00₹1,890.00
Jacket₹2,5005%₹125.00₹2,625.00
Jacket₹2,50118%₹450.18₹2,951.18
Kurta₹11,00018%₹1,980.00₹12,980.00

A garment priced exactly at ₹2,500 is still taxed at 5%, because the limit is "up to" that amount.

Which products does it cover?

The rule is for readymade garments, which sit in HSN chapters 61 (knitted) and 62 (not knitted). Fabrics, footwear and made-up articles have their own entries, so check the HSN of each item before applying this rule.

Before 22 September 2025

Earlier, 5% applied up to ₹1,000 per piece and 12% above that. Invoices dated before 22 September 2025 follow the old rates.
