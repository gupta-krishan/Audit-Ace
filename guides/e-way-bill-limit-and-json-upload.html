E-Way Bill: When Needed, Validity and JSON Upload

Short answer: an e-way bill is generally needed to move goods when the value of the consignment is more than ₹50,000. It carries invoice details (Part A) and transport details (Part B).

What the e-way bill needs

  • Part A: supplier and recipient GSTIN, place of delivery, invoice number and date, HSN, value and tax.
  • Part B: the vehicle number, or the transporter ID, and the distance.

Validity

For normal cargo, validity is generally one day for every 200 km of distance. Over-dimensional cargo has a shorter distance per day.

Uploading a JSON file

The official e-way bill portal lets you upload one JSON file containing many bills, which saves typing each one. The required JSON format can be updated by the portal, so test with a single bill first and compare with the sample on the portal.

Exceptions and state rules

Some goods and movements are exempt, and states have their own rules for movement inside the state. Check the portal for your case before dispatch.
