GST Tax Invoice: Mandatory Details and Format

Short answer: under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, a tax invoice must show the supplier's details, a unique invoice number and date, recipient details, HSN, description, quantity, value, tax rate and amount, place of supply and a signature.

Checklist

  • Supplier name, address and GSTIN.
  • A consecutive serial number of up to 16 characters, using letters, numbers, hyphen or slash, unique in the financial year.
  • Date of issue.
  • Recipient name, address and GSTIN if registered.
  • For an unregistered buyer, with taxable value of ₹50,000 or more: name, address, delivery address, and state name and code.
  • HSN code, description of goods, quantity and unit.
  • Total value, and taxable value after discount.
  • Rate and amount of CGST, SGST or IGST, and cess if any.
  • Place of supply, with the state name, when it differs from the recipient's location.
  • Whether tax is payable on reverse charge.
  • Signature or digital signature of the supplier or authorised person.

Copies for goods

An invoice for goods is issued in triplicate: original for the recipient, duplicate for the transporter and triplicate for the supplier.

E-invoicing
