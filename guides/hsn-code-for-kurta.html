HSN Code for Kurta and the Right GST Rate

Short answer: HSN 62114210 is the tariff line listed for women's and girls' cotton kurta and salwar sets. The right code for other kurtas depends on gender, fabric and whether it is a set, so confirm it before billing.

What decides the HSN code

  • Who it is for: women's and girls' garments are classified separately from men's.
  • Fabric: cotton, man-made fibre and silk fall under different sub-headings.
  • How it is made: knitted garments are in chapter 61 and woven garments in chapter 62.
  • Single piece or set: a kurta sold with a salwar is treated differently from a kurta sold alone.

Known listing

HSN lookup sites list 62114210 for women's and girls' cotton kurta and salwar sets, with or without dupatta. For men's kurtas, practice varies between shirt-type, suit-type and other-garment headings, so there is no single code to copy. Always confirm on the official GST portal or with your tax adviser.

GST rate on a kurta

Garment rates depend on price per piece: 5% up to ₹2,500 and 18% above. See the GST rate on clothes guide for examples.
